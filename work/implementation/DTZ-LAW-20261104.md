## Verified change

The ordinance amending the Integrationskurstestverordnung was signed on 13 October 2026 and promulgated on 20 October as **BGBl. 2026 I No. 306**. Article 1 replaces section 10(1); Article 2 makes it effective **1 November 2026**. The official Gazette PDF was retrieved and inspected; this is enacted law, not a proposal. Sources: [Gazette entry](https://www.recht.bund.de/bgbl/1/2026/306/VO.html), [enacted text](https://www.recht.bund.de/bgbl/1/2026/306/regelungstext.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1), [consolidated section 10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/inttestv/__10.html).

Overall DTZ B1 now requires all three conditions: B1 speaking, B1 in at least one of combined listening/reading and writing, and at least A2 in the remaining area. For example, B1 speaking plus B1 listening/reading plus writing below A2 does not yield overall B1. This closes the former possibility of compensating for a written area below A2. The rule is specific to DTZ; it must not be applied to telc Deutsch B1.

## Scope of the conclusion

This conclusion is an assessment of this specific amendment and the audited source behavior, not a certification of general legal compliance or of the live deployment. Official examination-centre duties and individual residence/citizenship eligibility are outside this assessment. Qualified content and language review obligations remain open; this review does not approve examination content.
